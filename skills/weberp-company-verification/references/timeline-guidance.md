APP SKILL: IndiaMART WebERP company verification

- The app commonly starts at the WebERP login page and may use Google sign-in plus OTP.
- Search is often performed from the main dashboard using a GLID or company identifier.
- Search results may visually look like a normal click target, but the company detail view is loaded through named frames.
- The useful post-search states often include company summary, KYC, GST/CIN status, and address verification.
- When visible, preserve domain labels like GLID, GST, CIN, and verification status because they are likely business outputs.
- Preserve structural terms like View More, Know Your Customer, Visiting Card, GST Verified, CIN Verified, and Address not Verified when they appear on screen.
- When two frames show related company content, distinguish them:
  - summary/header frames contain company metadata and expansion controls
  - detail/analytics frames contain KYC and category metrics
- Prefer final business verification states over intermediate decorative content such as ratings, badges, or social proof.
